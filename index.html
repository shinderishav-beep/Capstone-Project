Restore the Color · Be Yourself

Long ago, this world glowed with every color you can imagine. But little by little, people stopped expressing who they really were. They copied each other's clothes, repeated each other's opinions, and hid what made them different — until one morning, every color in the world faded to gray.

Only one spark of color remains: you.

Four magical crystals lie hidden across the land, each guarded by a character who remembers what it means to be yourself. Earn all four crystals and color will return — and along the way, your choices will reveal what kind of person you truly are.
