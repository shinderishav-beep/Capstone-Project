The Color Collector

A game about being yourself.

The world has turned gray. Everyone tried so hard to be the same as everyone else that all the color drained away — from the trees, the flowers, the houses, even the sky.

Four magical crystals are hidden across the land, each guarded by someone who still remembers what it means to be yourself. Find them, listen to their lessons, and bring the color back.

Move with Arrow keys / WASD. Talk to guardians with E (or Space/Enter).

The World Is Bright Again

Color has returned!

It was never the crystals that held the magic — it was the choices. A favorite color picked honestly. A path walked your own way. A look that's truly yours. A strength you're proud to name.

When people are brave enough to be themselves — to make their own choices, express who they are, and not be afraid of standing out — the whole world becomes a brighter, more colorful place.

Be proud of who you are. There is no one alive who is Youer than You.
